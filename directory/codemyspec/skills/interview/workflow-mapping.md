# Workflow mapping: the larger job

A workflow is one job a kind of customer does from a starting point to an
outcome someone can check. Stories are its steps. Map one workflow at a time.

1. Settle who it is for, where they start and what outcome ends it.
2. Ask the person to tell one real session from start to finish.
3. Cut it into steps wherever something changes that someone could verify
   ("payment clears and the account is active"). Confirm each step.
4. Each step becomes a story, or attaches to one that exists. A step may have no
   story yet while the backbone is being mapped.

A story names its workflow when you pass it to the manager; the project keeps
each workflow as a list of its stories in order.
